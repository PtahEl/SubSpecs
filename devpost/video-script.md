# SubSpecs: demo video script (draft, under 3 minutes)

Recorded as a screen capture of Lens Studio's Preview (the rules say reviewers won't run the project,
so the video must show it working end to end). Voice-over in plain words. Times are targets.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title card: SubSpecs logo, "Live subs for the conversation around you." | "If you're deaf or hard of hearing, a loud corridor or a hospital front desk means missing most of the conversation, or looking down at a phone and losing everyone's faces." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Start card in the Preview. Zoom on the privacy note. | "SubSpecs puts the words in front of you on SPECS, so your eyes stay on people. Subs are shown only to you, and SubSpecs saves no audio or text." |
| 0:25–1:05 | Pinch **Conference corridor**. Sam's voice plays, her card types in time; Daniel joins; his card shows "Daniel" once he says his name; Lucía speaks Spanish, "Spanish detected", English underneath. Drag a card beside a person. | "Each voice gets its own card in the demo. Words appear as they're spoken. When someone says their name, their card uses it. Lucía speaks Spanish, so it's translated automatically. I can drag a card next to whoever's talking, and the card of whoever is speaking comes forward." |
| 1:05–1:15 | Pinch **Recap**; the panel opens. | "Joined late? Recap sums up what everyone has said." |
| 1:15–2:00 | Pinch **Switch**, then **Hospital intake**. Yuki (Japanese), Carlos (Spanish), Amélie (French). Their answers are translated, the facts that matter are highlighted, and they fill the intake form as they're said. SubSpecs even suggests the next question, in English and in the patient's language, so the worker can read it out." |

| 2:35–2:55 | Lens Studio with the LEAF panel: tests passing; HISTORY.md scrolling. | "It was planned with the Devpost Learn skill pack and built by a coding agent with CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP. Twenty LEAF tests check every feature. The biggest lesson: don't fake what the glasses can't do." |
| 2:55–3:00 | Logo. | "SubSpecs. Take part in every conversation." |

## Capture notes
- Reset the Preview camera first so the view is the wearer's.
- Record Demo mode with sound on: the voices are part of the demo.
- For Live mode, the Preview can't reach Snap's gateway. Show the status bar and the stand-in test run
  (`live-mode` with `screenshot=true` stops with Lucía's line, translation, reply help and recap on
  screen), and say plainly that real transcription needs real SPECS.
- Don't show the reach test or anything flaky.
- Keep it under 3:00; cut the Recap beat first if long.
