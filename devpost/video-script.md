# SubSpecs: demo video (final, Nov 7, 2026)

**Watch:** https://youtu.be/Tm1-vk3SC84 (2:55)

Recorded as a screen capture of Lens Studio's Preview (Plane world) from a fresh clone of this repo,
then cut, zoomed in on the Lens so the subs are readable, and narrated. Narration, music and the demo
voices were generated with ElevenLabs. Nothing in the Lens footage was edited or added; section labels
and the title and end cards were added in the edit.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card: SubSpecs logo, "Live subs for the conversation around you" | "If you're deaf or hard of hearing, a loud corridor or a busy front desk means missing most of the conversation, or staring down at a phone and losing everyone's faces." |
| 0:11 | Start card with the privacy note | "SubSpecs puts live subs on Snap's SPECS glasses, right where you're already looking. Subs are shown only to you." |
| 0:19 | **Demo 1, Conference corridor:** Sam, Daniel and Lucía, one card each | "In the demo, every voice gets its own color-coded card, typing in time as they speak." / "When someone says their name, their card picks it up." / "Lucía speaks Spanish. SubSpecs detects it, and puts the English right underneath." |
| 1:18 | Switch, back to the start card, then Hospital intake | "Now, a hospital intake desk beside an international airport. The patients speak Japanese, Spanish and French." |
| 1:30 | **Demo 2, Hospital intake:** Yuki (Japanese), Carlos (Spanish), Amélie (French); facts highlighted, "Ask:" suggestions | "Each answer is translated into English, and the facts a nurse needs, like symptoms, pain and allergies, light up in green." / "SubSpecs even suggests the next question, in English and in the patient's language, so the worker can just read it out." |
| 2:28 | **Live mode:** the start card's Live button; the bar's status shows Live can't reach the AI in Preview | "Live mode listens through the glasses' microphone, and uses an AI model through Snap's own cloud, so it runs on real SPECS. The glasses can't tell voices apart yet, so Live mode uses one honest Speaking card instead of guessing." |
| 2:43 | End card: logo, "Take part in every conversation.", built with CLAD, Lens Studio MCP, LEAF | "Planned with the Devpost Learn skills, built with CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP, and tested with twenty LEAF scenarios. SubSpecs. Take part in every conversation." |
